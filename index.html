Designed to feel native to Todoist

A natural extension of your workflow that respects your privacy and enhances your productivity.

Multi-Tiered Horizon Planning

Connect long-term vision to immediate action. Every weekly milestone rolls up into a monthly focus, and every monthly focus feeds your yearly vision. When you check off a task, you feel the momentum toward what truly matters.

Native Sidebar Integration

Horizons lives right inside Todoist. Access it directly from your navigation sidebar, with a compact mini-calendar for instant date navigation and smooth transitions.

100% Private, Zero Telemetry

Your goals are personal. Horizons has zero analytics scripts, zero tracking pixels, and zero third-party telemetry. Everything stays stored securely in your browser.

Sync & Automated Backups

Keep your goals in sync across multiple computers using your existing Todoist account. Automatic hourly snapshots keep your planning safe with 1-click JSON export.

Light & Dark Noir Themes

Whether you plan in the daylight or reflect late at night, Horizons seamlessly adopts your active Todoist theme without any jarring color mismatches.
