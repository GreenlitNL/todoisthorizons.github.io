Horizons for Todoist

Plan and achieve your long-term goals across yearly, monthly, and weekly time horizons directly inside Todoist.

About the Extension

Horizons for Todoist brings multi-tiered horizon planning (Yearly Vision, Monthly Focus, and Weekly Milestones) seamlessly into your Todoist workflow. The extension operates locally in your browser with zero third-party telemetry, using your own Todoist account for optional multi-browser synchronization and automated backups.
