Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Executive Summary: Horizons for Todoist is built on a privacy-first, local-first architecture. We do not operate external analytics servers, we do not track your browsing, we do not collect personal identifiers, and we never sell or monetize user data. All goals stay on your machine and in your own private Todoist account.

1. Data Collection & Single Purpose

Horizons for Todoist ("the Extension", "we", "us") exists solely to provide horizon-based goal planning (Yearly, Monthly, and Weekly time horizons) within the Todoist web application. We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization and the Chrome Web Store Limited Use Requirements.

2. Where Your Data is Stored

Your horizon goals, time period selections, collapsed section preferences, and backup snapshots are stored locally on your device using Chrome's extension storage engine (chrome.storage.local).

This data remains sandboxed within your browser. It is never transmitted to any servers operated by the developers of this extension.

3. Synchronization & Backups via Todoist

If you choose to use the extension's cross-device synchronization or automated backups:

  • The Extension communicates directly and exclusively with official Todoist API endpoints (https://api.todoist.com/*) using your active Todoist session.
  • Synchronized goal records and backup comments are stored directly inside a private, hidden project within your own personal Todoist account (titled "Horizons sync (do not delete)").
  • No third-party intermediary servers ever see, intercept, or process your goal data.

4. Zero Telemetry, Analytics, or Tracking

The Extension contains zero third-party analytics scripts, zero tracking pixels, zero telemetry beacons, and zero advertising libraries.

  • We do not record what websites you visit.
  • We do not record the content of your tasks or personal notes.
  • We do not set tracking cookies.

5. User-Initiated Support & Feedback

If you voluntarily submit a message through the extension's built-in Support or Feedback forms:

  • The form data (your message, selected category/topic, and optional reply email) is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Web3Forms (https://api.web3forms.com/*) solely for delivery to our support team.
  • If the "Include system diagnostics" checkbox is selected, non-identifying technical metadata (extension version, browser platform, locale, and timestamp) is included to help troubleshoot your issue.
  • This information is used strictly to respond to your inquiry and is never shared, marketed, or sold.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally in your browser and in your own Todoist account:

  • Local Data: You can completely erase all local extension data at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing extension data in your browser settings.
  • Cloud Backups & Sync: You can view, restore, or permanently delete individual backups from the Backup Manager in the extension popup, or delete the sync project directly from your Todoist account.

7. Compliance & Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to the Extension or updates to regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue or inquiry via our official repository at GitHub.
